Support for a changing world.
With advancements in medical technology and research, there’s never been a more exciting time to be involved in hematology.  
As pioneers in our field, our role is to lead the change and find novel ways to incorporate new techniques and therapies into daily practice. We need to rapidly deploy these innovations to fuel our advance towards prevention, a cure, and improved quality of life.
## Overview
EHA launched the Topics-in-Focus program to provide support and guidance to hematologists and amplify efforts by focusing on a specific disease related or area related topics to provide better access to innovation.
The program is designed to:
  * Raise awareness
  * Provide education
  * Develop further research frameworks and access

## What is precision hematology?
Precision hematology is an integrated approach to diagnosing and treating blood disorders—particularly hematologic malignancies—by leveraging detailed molecular, genetic, and functional information about each patient’s disease.
It matches therapies more precisely to individual patients by uniting innovative clinical trial designs with cutting-edge diagnostics, such as:
  * Advanced imaging
  * Genetic profiling
  * Drug-sensitivity testing
This field brings together physicians, scientists, and other key stakeholders across preclinical, translational, and clinical research. Through this collaboration, precision hematology aims to:
  * Accelerate the discovery of new treatment strategies
  * Refine therapeutic decision-making
  * Improve outcomes for patients with blood cancers
## Our program’s goals
Our Precision Hematology Topics-in-Focus program is designed to promote innovative concepts for diagnostics and innovative clinical trial designs which will feed into the clinical application of precision hematology.
The goal of the program is to create a European regulatory framework for precision hematology. By increasing awareness and providing education, guidelines, and policies, we can improve access to:
  * Diagnostics
  * Clinical research
  * Treatment in malignant hematology

**EHA joins forces with the British Society of Haematology (BSH) & Annual Academy of Sickle Cell and Thalassaemia Conference (ASCAT) in organizing the 19th Annual Sickle Cell & Thalassaemia Conference.**
**Dates:** October 2-5, 2024  
**Location:** London, United Kingdom  
**Format:** Physical only  
The Annual Scientific Conference on Sickle Cell and Thalassaemia (ASCAT) focuses on promoting the latest advances in diagnosis, treatment, and emerging fields in hemoglobinopathies. It is an opportunity to interact with peers on the latest advances in clinical care, transition services, and emerging new therapies including updates for curative treatment options.
### Abstract submission
Abstract submission is an important part of this meeting. As a participant, you will have the opportunity to submit an abstract for this meeting, and (if accepted) have the possibility to discuss it with the international faculty and your peers as an oral presentation or poster.
Please select the most appropriate topic during the abstract & case submission:
  1. Basic and translational
  2. Novel therapies, gene therapies, bone marrow transplant and emerging diagnostics
  3. Clinical and epidemiological studies
  4. Infection, autoimmunity, nutritional deficiencies
  5. Ageing and end organ damage
  6. Health services and outcomes research including psychology
Abstract submission closed
