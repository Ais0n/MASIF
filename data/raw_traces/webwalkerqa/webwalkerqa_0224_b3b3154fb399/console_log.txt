# Legal Jibber Jabber
!
Last Updated: August 2018
We love our players and the awesome stuff they create. We support players who use our intellectual property (“IP”) to create free fan projects for the benefit of the community (“Projects”). We’re generally on board with Projects that follow the rules outlined below, but we can still shut down any Project at any time if we decide it misinterprets these rules or uses our IP inappropriately.
### 1. What can I develop with Riot’s IP?
TL;DR - Cool free stuff for the community to enjoy, with some exceptions.
By formal decree of the Demacian High Court (our legal team): on the condition that you follow all of the rules described in this policy (the “Rules”) and the , Riot Games (“Riot” or “We”) grants you a personal, non-exclusive, non-sublicenseable, non-transferable, revocable, limited license for you to use, display and create derivative works based upon Riot’s IP, strictly for noncommercial (except as specifically provided below) community use. We reserve the right to deny anyone the use of our IP at any time, for any reason or no reason, including when we decide, in our sole and absolute discretion, that you are using our IP inappropriately. If we deny you the right to use our IP, you must stop developing, publishing, or distributing your Project immediately.
### 2. No commercial projects (except…)
TL;DR - We allow passive ad revenue generation for some content, subscriptions and donations on streaming channels, and some commercial projects that comply with our API Terms and Policies and use a valid API key
You may not create commercial Projects, including any Project that crowdsources any portion of its funding, any Project that involves a business or legal entity, or any Project where you gate the content with a paywall (e.g., Patreon, YouTube Premium, etc.) without a written license agreement from us. We have only three exceptions.
#### The Three Exceptions
##### Exception 1: Ad Revenue
We permit individual players to promote their Projects on websites, streams, or videos and passively generate revenue through appropriate advertisements, including pre-roll ads, ad breaks, and sponsor ad overlays. No inappropriate ads—we alone decide what qualifies as inappropriate.
##### Exception 2: Gameplay Streaming
We permit individual players to solicit personal donations or offer subscription-based content while live-streaming games, so long as non-subscribers can still watch the games concurrently.
##### Exception 3: API Dev Terms and Policies
We permit commercial Projects that both (1) comply with our  and ; and (2) use a currently valid Riot API key that we granted to you specifically for your Project. We reserve the right to distribute and revoke API keys in our sole and absolute discretion. If we revoke your API key for any reason, you must immediately shut down your Project. 
### 3. No unauthorized games or apps.
We prohibit the use of our IP in games and apps. Please do not take any part of our IP (e.g., character appearance, character abilities, maps, icons, items, etc.) and use it in a game or app. To be super clear: we do not allow any Projects on the Apple Store or Google Play Store unless they have either a written license agreement from us or a valid Riot API key and comply with our API Terms and Policies (described above).
### 4. Does my Project have to include original content?
TL;DR - Yes. Please don’t just rip off existing stuff. Make something new!
  1. You may not use a third party’s IP in your Project without their permission.
  2. Don’t steal other people’s Projects; get their permission and give them credit.
  3. Don’t just rip off or add some light commentary to existing content (e.g., esports matches, other players’ vods). Please make your own, original contribution to the community. We reserve the right to use services that identify our content automatically to prevent cannibalization of existing content.
### 5. Can I use Riot’s logos or trademarks?
TL;DR - No, we don’t want players to be confused about any possible connection between Riot and your Project.
Unless you have a written license agreement with us, you may not use any of our logos or trademarks anywhere in your Project or on any website, advertising material, video, or other publication. You may not register domain names, social media accounts, or similar stuff that uses Riot Games or any of our trademarks, trade names, character names, etc. You may not use our trademarks or names related to our IP as keywords or internet search tags. 
### 6. Can I share my Project with the community?
TL;DR - Defo. Just let people know it’s a fan Project, not a Riot project.
If you share your Project with others, please conspicuously include the following notice (e.g., on your Project’s website):
[The title of your Project] was created under Riot Games' "Legal Jibber Jabber" policy using assets owned by Riot Games. Riot Games does not endorse or sponsor this project.
### 7. Can Riot use my Project?
TL;DR - Yes.
We want fans to create and share cool things with each other, and we want to remove barriers to sharing. If we spotlight a fan Project or make something that resembles what a fan might have made, we don’t want to be sued, so you give us permission to spotlight or share your Project with the world. More specifically: you agree that we may use, copy, modify, distribute, and make derivative works of your Project in any form, on a royalty-free, non-exclusive, irrevocable, transferable, sub-licensable, worldwide basis, for any purpose and without having to credit you, pay you anything, or obtain your approval.
* * *
So to wrap this up, here’s the summary: just follow these Rules so you don’t upset Corporate Mundo, and keep your Project free for the community to enjoy.

Riot Games, Inc. is a global gaming company headquartered in Los Angeles with offices and operations around the world. Riot Games, Inc. and its subsidiaries take privacy as seriously as we do play. This Candidate & B2B Privacy Notice (“Notice”) explains what Personal Info we collect during our application and recruitment process, our business to business (B2B) relationships, info about your rights, as well as ways we may use or disclose Personal Info.
By “Personal Info,” we mean any information from or about an identified or identifiable person, including information that Riot Games can associate with an individual person. When we say “Riot Games” (or similar things like “we” or “us”), we’re referring to the Riot Games entity or, where applicable, entities that control and are responsible for your Personal Info.
We have developed this Notice to comply with applicable data protection laws globally. To the extent this Notice conflicts with local laws in your jurisdiction, local laws apply. This Candidate & B2B Privacy Notice applies to info: 
  * You disclose to us directly (like by submitting an application or talking to our recruiters and interviewers or, where you work for one of our suppliers, customers, partners, agents or investors, when you give it to one of your Riot Games contacts); and 
  * We obtain from others or public sources in connection with:
    * Our recruitment efforts, such as from background checking agencies (where used as permitted by local laws), external talent database holders (who may collect your info from you, third parties they work with, or from public sources), executive search and recruitment agencies, and from publicly available sources, including any social media platforms you use or other info available online like LinkedIn;
    * B2B relationships, such as where the company you work for gives it to us (like where you are a contact for services being provided to us).

The particular kinds of Personal Info we gather often depend on how you interact with us and, for candidates, the role for which you’re being considered. These include:
  * _Contact Info._ We collect names, email addresses, telephone numbers, and similar business or personal contact info to help us communicate with you.

  * _Immigration/visa status._ We collect details of your immigration or visa status.

However, in some circumstances, and where local laws allow, we may collect or request some Sensitive Personal Info for legitimate employment-related purposes where necessary. For example, Personal Info about your racial/ethnic origin, gender, date of birth, and disabilities, as well as photographs, for the purposes of equal opportunities monitoring and diversity and inclusion efforts.
## 3. How We Use Personal Info
We collect Personal Info to support our application and recruitment process and our B2B relationships, including to: 
  * Process your application (such as adding your Personal Info to our systems);
  * Communicate with you (like to schedule interview times and dates or in connection with services provided);
  * Assess your eligibility for our available opportunities or to enter into a contract (including other current or potential future openings at Riot Games you may not have considered);
  * Measure and improve our recruitment process (like by analyzing hiring trends and enhancing our diversity efforts usually by aggregating your data with others before analyzing it);
  * Comply with our legal and regulatory obligations (such as submitting required reports, and conducting risk management assessments for B2B relationships);
  * Verify the Personal Info you submit (including, where applicable, by conducting employment history, background, and reference checks);

  * Perform our business contract with you or where applicable, our business customer, supplier, partner, agent or investor whom you act on behalf of or represent; and
  * Conduct and develop our business (such as managing our business relationships including account management, business development and marketing in relation to our existing and prospective business suppliers, customers, partners, agents or investors).
We’ll also use Personal Info as we believe is necessary or appropriate under applicable law to protect the rights and property of Riot Games, players, other candidates, our employees, or the public.

If you are not successful, we may still keep your application to allow us to consider you for other suitable openings within Riot Games in the future and will delete your CV, résumé, and cover letter in line with local retention requirements. Please inform us if you do not want us to do so.
If you work for one of our suppliers, customers, partners, agents or investors, the processing is necessary for our legitimate interests, namely conducting and developing our business.

As part of the B2B relationship, we disclose your Personal Info with security operations, the legal team, purchasing operations, finance, and those directly involved in the ongoing B2B services. In each case, these individuals may be spread across various offices and locations worldwide. We disclose the Personal Info we collect with our subsidiaries and affiliates, which means your Personal Info could be transferred to or stored in the US, UK, or other locations outside your country of residence.
We use service providers to support our recruitment efforts and B2B relationships. This includes:
  * recruitment or executive search agencies involved in your recruitment; 
  * data storage, shared services and recruitment platform providers, IT developers and support providers, and providers of hosting services; and
  * third parties who provide support and advice including in relation to immigration, pre-engagement screening, legal, financial / audit, management consultancy, insurance, health and safety, security and intel, and whistleblowing / reporting issues.
With your consent, we may also utilize an artificial intelligence tool service provider for real-time transcription and note-taking during interviews, so that our interviewers can give you their full attention. Any such tools would not be used for decision-making.
We require these service providers to protect the Personal Info they receive with appropriate security measures and prohibit them from using the Personal Info for their own purposes or outside what is necessary to provide the service. These service providers may be located outside your country of residence or the country where the position for which you applied is located. 
We’ll disclose Personal Info to third parties where we have a legitimate interest in doing so or where otherwise permitted by law.
