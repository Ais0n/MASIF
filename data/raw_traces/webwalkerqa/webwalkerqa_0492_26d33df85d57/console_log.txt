# Annual Meeting Knowledge Bowl
!
Get ready for a fun, fast-paced, and competitive session based on the game show, _Jeopardy!_ Each year, rheumatology fellows from around the world are challenged with questions on a wide range of rheumatic and musculoskeletal disease topics. Just like _Jeopardy!_ , contestants are required to provide their answers in the form of a question. Teams compete against other universities and institutions for exciting prizes, including complimentary registration for the upcoming annual meeting.
#### Knowledge Bowl Call for Teams
The Knowledge Bowl call for teams is open. Please review the  prior to applying.
**Submission Deadline:** June 9, noon ET
### Knowledge Bowl Details
## What types of questions are asked?
Questions consist of disease-specific or broad topics relevant to rheumatology.
## What are the eligibility requirements?
Each team must have a team name and include one (1) attending and two (2) fellows. Each team member must participate in ACR Convergence 2026 to be eligible to participate in the Knowledge Bowl. If you do not plan to participate in ACR Convergence in person, please do not sign up for the Knowledge Bowl.
## What is the cost of registration?
The ACR Knowledge Bowl is included as part of general registration. All team members must be registered for ACR Convergence to be eligible to participate in the Knowledge Bowl. Team members are responsible for the costs of their registration.
## When will I know if my team has been accepted?
Teams will be notified of their acceptance June 12.
## How do I submit a question to be included in the Knowledge Bowl?
To gather quality questions for each category, we have formally invited faculty to be the official Question Writers for the Knowledge Bowl. Each invited Question Writer will gain access to our submission site to provide their entries. If you are interested in being a Question Writer and would like to be considered, please contact ACR staff at facultymanagement@rheumatology.org.

# Annual Meeting Thieves Market
!
Share your most interesting case images with rheumatologists from around the world during the Thieves Market. We are looking for rare or fascinating cases with dynamic imagery. Selection emphasis is placed on pictorial displays of interesting physical findings or imaging studies, and each case should include a brief introduction and summary for presentation purposes. Cases are selected for both oral and poster presentation.
ACR will offer two Thieves Market sessions - one for adult cases and one for pediatric cases. The ACR Convergence 2026 first place winners in both categories will receive complimentary registration to ACR Convergence 2027 and will be featured in _ACR Open Rheumatology_ as a Brief and/or Case Report.
#### Thieves Market Submission Instructions
Thieves Market submission is open. Please note the following:
✓ All submissions must be made online
✓ Visit the online submission site to get started
✓ Read the complete  prior to submitting a case
**Submission Deadline:** June 9, noon ET _(no exceptions)_
**Note:** No changes may be made to your submission, including author information, after the June 9 deadline.
### Case Submission Requirements
Submit a truly unique case with an extremely high educational value.
You may submit one (1) case.
Presentations must be in PowerPoint© format.
DO NOT include your name, institution/affiliation name, or logo in your case slides. The judging process is blinded to eliminate any potential bias.
Cases can consist of no more than ten (10) slides and must be formatted as follows:
    * Slide 1 must be your title slide. It should include the case title only. DO NOT include your name, institution/affiliation name, or logo.
    * Slides 2–10 must be your case content slides. These should include all images and data pertinent to your case. Your content slides should tell the story of your case.
    * Although the emphasis will be on pictorial displays of interesting physical findings or imaging studies, each case should have a brief introduction, a summary at the end of the presentation, and an audience response question. These items should be included as part of your case content slides (slides 2-10).
