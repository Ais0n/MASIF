EHA’s Specialized Working Groups (SWGs) foster science and spread knowledge in basic, translational, and clinical research in hematology.
To ensure we cover the full field of hematology and provide a research platform for all groups and researchers, we’ve established two types of SWG:
  * **Disease-oriented SWGs** —which cover specific topics such as leukemias and lymphomas

#  SWG Grants 
As part of our commitment to being the leading force in hematology, EHA provides financial support to small research projects initiated by our Specialized Working Groups (SWGs).
Every year, we award grants to projects that will:
  * Help us build a collaborative network
  * Increase engagement within SWGs
## What an SWG Grant can cover
SWG Grants can cover costs related to:
  * Data management
  * Data analysis
  * Sample collections
  * Storage
They cannot be used to:
  * Support fellowships
  * Pay individual salaries
  * Purchase substantial equipment
## How many grants will be avarded per this funding cycle?
Up to 5 SWG grants, each up to 30,000 EUR
## Eligibility
Non-interventional studies, like retrospective studies or prospective data collection, are eligible for support.
Applications for basic research projects are also eligible. In these cases, we favor those that:
  * Take advantage of the collaborative nature of the SWG network
  * Encourage the mutual exchange of expertise
To increase diversity and support inclusion within our SWG community, we encourage proposals that involve:
  * Junior members
  * Partners from underrepresented countries
In all cases, the principal investigator(s) (PIs) in the application must be both an:
  * EHA member
  * SWG member
All applications should be communicated internally inside your SWG.
There’s no limit on the number of applications that an SWG can submit.
## Selection
Winning grant applications are selected by the .
In making its decision, the committee considers:
• The scientific quality of the project  
• The importance of the topic for further research/subsequent projects  
• The explanation of the budget needs  
• Cooperation/interaction within the SWG or with other SWGs  
• Other sources of support that are already available for this project  
• The overlap with ongoing/already funded projects  
• The inclusion of junior members and/or partners from underrepresented countries
## How to apply
To apply, complete the application form below and send it to .
The **SWG Chair** must submit the application, or they must be copied in on your application email.
The call for SWG-research initiated grants closed on October 17, 2025. The awardees list will be published on the SWG grants webpage. The next call will open in summer 2026.
