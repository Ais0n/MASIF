Instead, written questions will be collected during and after the presentation and delivered to the session chair, who will then consolidate, summarize, and paraphrase them for the keynote speaker to answer. Students will be periodically walking up and down the aisles with a small sign indicating they are collecting questions starting approximately 1/2 hour after the beginning of the keynote speaker's address. They will have note cards to write the questions on, or you may submit the question on any note paper.
