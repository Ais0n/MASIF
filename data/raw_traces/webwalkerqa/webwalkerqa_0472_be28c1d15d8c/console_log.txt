# Encore!
Encore AURORA concerts begin on August 23rd and run through September 3rd PDT (UTC -7). AURORA will appear in Home, offering to teleport you directly to the Coliseum.
On the first three days of the encore, from August 23rd through 25th (Wed.-Fri.), the concert will be held on a special schedule starting every **eight** hours: 3:00 pm, 11:00 pm, and 7:00 am **CEST** (that’s 6:00 am, 2:00 pm, and 10:00 pm PDT).
Then from August 26th though September 3rd, the concert will begin every **four** hours starting at 11:00 am **CEST** (2:00 am PDT).
