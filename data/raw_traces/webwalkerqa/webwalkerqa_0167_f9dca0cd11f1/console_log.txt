# Annual Meetings
!
### Advancing collaboration at the cutting edge of scientific discovery
MRS Meetings serve as an international stage for the examination and dissemination of current and emerging materials research. At the heart of the Meetings portfolio are the Spring and Fall Meetings. Researchers from all scientific fields, backgrounds and employment sectors—from students to Nobel Laureates—converge at these events to exchange technical information, engage in broader impact programming, network with friends and colleagues and contribute to the advancement of materials research. Large-scale exhibits add to the interactive Meeting experience, as companies from around the world present innovative products and services to the scientific community, and in turn, gather information to develop new products and partnership that drive innovation.
While Spring and Fall Meetings are at the core of the Meetings portfolio, MRS continues to enhance its service to the global materials community by endorsing and co-sponsoring standalone technical workshops and conferences.  
### Upcoming Annual Meetings
The MRS Spring and Fall Meetings are essential for discovering and presenting the latest developments in materials research. The Fall Meeting, held annually in Boston, Massachusetts, is a premier event in the field. These meetings reflect the growing global, cross-disciplinary collaboration in materials research each year.
](
### 
The 2026 MRS Spring Meeting & Exhibit, held April 26–May 1 in Honolulu, Hawai‘i, brought together materials researchers from academia, industry, government and national laboratories for a week of cross-disciplinary collaboration and scientific exchange.
](
### 
The 2026 MRS Fall Meeting & Exhibit will take place November 29-December 5, 2026 in Boston, Massachusetts, at the Hynes Convention Center and adjacent Sheraton Boston Hotel.
](
### 
From April 5–9, 2027, researchers worldwide will gather in Seattle, Washington for the MRS Spring Meeting to share advances across disciplines, exchange technical insights, and explore the future of materials science and innovation.
](
### 
The 2027 MRS Fall Meeting & Exhibit will take place November 28-December 3, 2025 in Boston, Massachusetts, at the Hynes Convention Center and adjacent Sheraton Boston Hotel.
!
### Upcoming Spring Meetings
Future Spring Meetings will be held in dynamic, globally connected cities that offer welcoming settings for collaboration, discovery, and scientific exchange. 
2026 — Honolulu, Hawai‘i — April 26–May 1 
2027 — Seattle, Washington — April 5–9 
**2028 — Vancouver, British Columbia — March 27–31**
2029 — Seattle, Washington — April 9-13
2030 — Honolulu, Hawai‘i — April 28-May 3
### Participating in MRS Spring and Fall Meetings
#### Symposium Proposals
  * Proposals for symposia at MRS Meetings are solicited approximately 18 months in advance of a Meeting. Information on when and how to propose will be made available on each Meeting's respective home page.  
  
  * MRS requires a diversified group of four co-organizers with respect to gender and under-represented groups, region, emphasis (fundamental vs applied), and institution. Inclusion of translational research and industrial involvement is highly encouraged, as is participation of early-career/postdoc co-organizers.  
  
  * .
#### Abstract Submission
  * Abstract submission for an MRS Spring or Fall Meeting occurs approximately 5-6 months prior to the Meeting. Information and timelines can be found on each Meeting's respective home page or the Call for Papers page.  
  
  * Call for papers are posted approximately 10 months in advance of a Meeting.

# Mark Your Calendars!
The **2026 MRS Fall Meeting & Exhibit** returns to the vibrant city of Boston, Massachusetts, from November 29 – December 4, 2026, bringing together the global materials research community for a week of innovation, collaboration, and discovery. Held at the Hynes Convention Center and the adjacent Sheraton Boston Hotel, this premier international scientific gathering will showcase the latest advancements in materials science, from quantum materials to energy and sustainability.
As the must-attend event for researchers, industry professionals, and students, the 2026 MRS Fall Meeting will feature a dynamic mix of cutting-edge presentations, interactive exhibits, and invaluable networking opportunities. Attendees will engage in thought-provoking discussions, explore groundbreaking technologies, and contribute to the ever-evolving field of materials research.
With a rich program designed to foster collaboration and inspire new ideas, the 2026 MRS Fall Meeting & Exhibit is the ideal platform to connect with leading minds, share research, and drive the future of materials science. Don’t miss this opportunity to be at the forefront of scientific innovation in Boston!

# Programs
Broadening our impact within and outside the materials community
The Materials Research Society has developed a far-reaching series of programs that expose students, teachers, and life-long learners to the excitement of discovery and the significance of science in their daily lives. For interested MRS members, these programs provide an opportunity to participate in outreach activities, support national STEM education initiatives, and play a significant role in developing and nurturing the next generation of materials scientists.

Broadening Participation in Materials
Broadening Participation in Materials
MRS recognizes that broad perspectives drive innovation, excellence and new discoveries. Our Society is committed to creating an inclusive and welcoming environment where all Members can impact the future of materials research and have equal access to career advancement opportunities through education, recognition for their research, and networking within our global materials community. By broadly engaging our diverse global membership, we will continue to advance more creative and innovative outcomes in the field of Materials Science and Engineering.

Featured Broadening Participation in Materials Programs
The MRS Student Science Writing Program offers students an opportunity to work with MRS Bulletin staff to develop communication skills and write MRS news articles, blogs, social media posts, or other communications.
**MRS PREM Research Scholars Summit**
This program offers student scholars the chance to engage with materials researchers and meet key stakeholders within the materials community. Students can take part is a variety of mentoring, leadership, and professional development sessions, and present their research contributions to the broader scientific community.
**Childcare Grants**
The Materials Research Society has a limited number of  available for those attending the annual Meetings. Grants of up to $300 will be given to selected registered meeting attendees who are bringing young children or who incur extra expenses in leaving them at home (i.e., added hours of daycare or babysitting services).

# Programs
Broadening our impact within and outside the materials community
The Materials Research Society has developed a far-reaching series of programs that expose students, teachers, and life-long learners to the excitement of discovery and the significance of science in their daily lives. For interested MRS members, these programs provide an opportunity to participate in outreach activities, support national STEM education initiatives, and play a significant role in developing and nurturing the next generation of materials scientists.
!
Broadening Participation in Materials
Broadening Participation in Materials
MRS recognizes that broad perspectives drive innovation, excellence and new discoveries. Our Society is committed to creating an inclusive and welcoming environment where all Members can impact the future of materials research and have equal access to career advancement opportunities through education, recognition for their research, and networking within our global materials community. By broadly engaging our diverse global membership, we will continue to advance more creative and innovative outcomes in the field of Materials Science and Engineering.

Featured Broadening Participation in Materials Programs
The MRS Student Science Writing Program offers students an opportunity to work with MRS Bulletin staff to develop communication skills and write MRS news articles, blogs, social media posts, or other communications.
**MRS PREM Research Scholars Summit**
This program offers student scholars the chance to engage with materials researchers and meet key stakeholders within the materials community. Students can take part is a variety of mentoring, leadership, and professional development sessions, and present their research contributions to the broader scientific community.
**Childcare Grants**
The Materials Research Society has a limited number of  available for those attending the annual Meetings. Grants of up to $300 will be given to selected registered meeting attendees who are bringing young children or who incur extra expenses in leaving them at home (i.e., added hours of daycare or babysitting services).

# Programs

The Materials Research Society has developed a far-reaching series of programs that expose students, teachers, and life-long learners to the excitement of discovery and the significance of science in their daily lives. For interested MRS members, these programs provide an opportunity to participate in outreach activities, support national STEM education initiatives, and play a significant role in developing and nurturing the next generation of materials scientists.

Broadening Participation in Materials
MRS recognizes that broad perspectives drive innovation, excellence and new discoveries. # MRS Shared Interest Groups
MRS Shared Interest Groups (SIGs) are communities within the Society with a shared interest in networking, community building, and passion for advancing the materials community. SIG members cooperate to support members’ careers and stimulate improvements in the materials community by informing each other, advising the Society, and, potentially, organizing activities or programs. All are welcome and encouraged to participate!
!
Current Shared Interest Groups are as follows:  
  * Awards Nominations
  * Higher Education  
  * LGBTQ+
  * Researchers of Color
  * Women in Materials Science and Engineering
Interested in learning more?
Log in to  , review your email preferences and opt-in to Shared Interest Group updates.
### More Information on MRS SIGs
Participation in MRS SIGs is open to all members and there are no set term requirements or limits, allowing members the flexibility to participate as their time and interest permits. SIGs have no ongoing charges or work duties from MRS, but are instead intended to build networking opportunities to advance member careers by providing a space for community-building and facilitating connections to all MRS programs and services.
The MRS  Broadening Participation in Materials Coordination Group (BPMCG) is the volunteer body that serves as the umbrella for existing SIGs. Their role is to maintain a high-level view of the SIGs and promote communication and potential collaboration among SIGs where appropriate to ensure balance among SIG activities in order to maximize MRS’s efforts to Broaden Participation in Materials and in the Society. The BPMCG can also provide guidance and input for new activities proposed by the SIGs prior to a formal approval review. The BPMCG may hold joint periodic meetings of the SIG leaders to assist in coordination of efforts.
MRS SIGs are intended to be self-organized by the SIG members, although MRS will provide logistical support setting up periodic SIG meetings, assisting with room and audio visual arrangements for SIG events (virtual or in person), and catalyzing connections to operating committees. 
SIGs that wish to propose an activity that requires funding may do so through the Broadening Participation in Materials Coordination Group Chair and Career Advancement Committee Chair for consideration. SIGs may also propose MRS Spring or Fall Meeting symposia through the normal . 
!
Program Assessment
MRS routinely assesses programs and services to ensure alignment of volunteer efforts, staff time and resources with MRS’s Strategic Plan, MRS’s Core Values, member preferences and community needs. At the highest level, it is MRS’s desire that our programs and services are aligned with our Aspiration.

###### 2026 MRS Spring Meeting & Exhibit
# Student Opportunities
Discover how MRS supports students at the 2026 Spring Meeting & Exhibit. Scroll down to explore exciting roles like **Meeting Scene Reporters and Symposium Assistants** , each designed to help you network, sharpen your science‑communication skills, and enrich your professional journey.
### MRS Meeting Scene—Call for Reporters and Bloggers
Graduate students and post-docs who are interested in contributing to the 2026 MRS Spring  are encouraged to apply.
Reporters will be required to attend talks in a variety of symposia and write brief summaries (100-250 words) of four talks each day; bloggers will be required to post at least five items per day and also tweet about their experiences at the Meeting. Reporters and Bloggers will have their registration reimbursed up to the Student Rate. In addition, volunteers will receive a stipend of $100.
##### To apply
Please send an email to newseditor@mrs.org stating your qualifications and your reasons for wanting to report or blog for us. We only need eight volunteers, so we will not be able to accept everyone who applies. Apply now! We look forward to hearing from you.
* * *
### Symposium Assistant Positions
Graduate students who are interested in assisting in the symposium sessions during the 2026 MRS Spring Meeting & Exhibit are encouraged to apply for a Symposium Assistant position. 
Symposium Assistants deliver essential Meeting materials to the technical session rooms, monitor audio-visual equipment and room lighting, track and record attendance and perform other tasks requested by the Session Chairs.
By assisting in a minimum of two days (four half-day sessions), students will receive a post-Meeting refund on their student registration, a one-year MRS student membership, and limited financial assistance to help defray expenses. 
Thank you for your interest in the Symposium Assistant Program. We are no longer accepting applications for the 2026 MRS Spring Meeting. Please check back later as spots may open up closer to the Meeting.  
  
If you have any questions, please contact **symposium-assistant@mrs.org**.
