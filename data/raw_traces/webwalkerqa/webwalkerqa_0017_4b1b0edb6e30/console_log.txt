# Kingdom Rush 5: Alliance
**A new evil emerges, and a formidable Alliance between Linirea’s bravest Heroes and the Dark Army must rise to defend the kingdom!**  
  
Fight unknown threats and wild enemies across colorful terrains in epic tower defense battles, using the best of both armies to boost your defense strategy.

Defend your ground with Kingdom Rush’s signature towers like precise Archers, valiant Paladins, and even trickful Demon Pits; or discover mighty newcomers like the Dwarven Flamespitter or the Eldritch Channelers.
Kingdom Rush 5: Alliance brings forth a fresh experience, taking the lightning-paced and captivating tower defense battles from its predecessors to the next level.
