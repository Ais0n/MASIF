## ION Meetings
  
## ION Meetings
### 
ION’s winter meeting is a more intimate conference with a technical program related to positioning, navigation and timing and includes the ION Fellows and Annual Awards presentations. 
### 
The Pacific PNT Conference is a global cooperative development of Positioning, Navigation and Timing technology where policy and technical leaders from Japan, Singapore, China, South Korea, Australia, USA and more meet to discuss policy updates, receive program status updates and exchange technical information. 
### 
This biennial conference co‐sponsored by the IEEE, AESS and the ION has a technical program that includes an emphasis on commercial inertial navigation. A commercial exhibit and pre-conference tutorials is held in conjunction with the conference. 
### 
The ION Military Division’s Joint Navigation Conference is the largest U.S. military navigation conference with joint service and government participation. The event focuses on technical advances in Guidance, Navigation, and Control (GN&C) with emphasis on joint development, test and support of affordable GN&C systems, logistics and integration. The conference includes a technical exhibit and showcase of Guidance, Navigation and Control technology products and services and Operational Product Demonstrations. 
### 
ION GNSS+ is the world’s largest international technical meeting and showcase of GNSS technology, products and services and brings together international leaders in GNSS and related positioning, navigation and timing fields to present advances, introduce new technologies, update current policy, demonstrate products and exchange ideas. 
### 
PTTI is an annual conference sponsored by ION with a technical program designed to disseminate and coordinate PTTI information at the user level, review present and future PTTI requirements, inform government and industry engineers, technicians, and managers of precise time and frequency technology and its problems, and provide an opportunity for an active exchange of new technology associated with PTTI. A commercial exhibit and pre-conference tutorials is held in conjunction with the conference.

### Register Your Staff
All exhibit personnel working your booth will be required to be registered for ITM/PTTI 2022. As part of the exhibit package, each company receives **one** full complimentary registration (excludes tutorials) for the complete technical meeting with each 10' x 10' booth purchased. Email registration@ion.org the name of your intended attendee to register any complimentary attendees. Any additional personnel will need to register for an exhibit hall only pass or for the full conference, using the online registration system. 
##### **Why We Charge for Exhibit Hall Passes**
The goal of show management is to provide our exhibitors with the maximum amount of unopposed exhibit time possible, to maximize face-to-face networking between our exhibitors and their customers. We do this by offering all of our meals and refreshments in the exhibit hall – you will find morning and afternoon coffee and snacks served exclusively in the exhibit hall, as well as a daily lunch buffet. These meal functions are included in the cost of your exhibitor pass.

### Update Your Online Profile- Be Seen
Engage and communicate with our virtual attendees through the expanded virtual meeting portal! The portal offers: 
  * Opt-In Attendee Directory
  * Real-time direct person-to-person messaging
  * **Chat Rooms**
  * Expanded Exhibitor Listing: You can personalize your expanded exhibitor profile by: 
    * Uploading a logo
    * Adding a company description
    * Providing contact information
    * Uploading electronic brochures.
    * Record an exhibitor demonstration to highlight your company’s product and services. This is a great opportunity to reach all attendees and to introduce your sales and engineering team. Send a link (youtube, etc.) for your company video to mandrews@ion.org, and have it added to your exhibitor profile!
Access to edit the exhibitor profile is automatically given to the contact person on the exhibit contract. To grant access to additional individuals, please email Megan Andrews at mandrews@ion.org. 
To edit your exhibitor profile, follow these steps:
  1. Log into your personal ION web account (click "sign in" at the top of the page). if you do not have an ION web account, you will need to create one; choose "create account" on the sign in page.
  2. Click on “My Account” at the top of the page.
  3. Under “My Exhibit Hall Profiles,” click on the appropriate meeting to edit your exhibitor profile.
  4. If you need technical assistance, please email Megan Andrews at mandrews@ion.org.
