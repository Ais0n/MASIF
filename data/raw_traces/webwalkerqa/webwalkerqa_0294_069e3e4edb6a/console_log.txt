# More Games, More Impact: Rovio Joins Forces with SpecialEffect for One Special Day
For the first time ever, three Rovio games are participating in One Special Day, a campaign in which all UK in-app purchases are donated to SpecialEffect to help people with disabilities play games.
Article Sustainability default 30.09.2025
We’ve all experienced it. The rush of launching a perfect Red, the satisfying pop of a pig structure collapsing, the joy of a high score. Games have a unique power to bring us together, to challenge us, and to simply make us smile. They are a universal language of fun and a source of connection for millions of people around the world. But for some, the simple act of holding a controller or tapping a screen can be a significant barrier.
Everyone, regardless of their physical abilities, should have the opportunity to experience the joy of play. This is why we are incredibly proud to announce our participation in this year’s One Special Day. And this year, we’re doing more than ever before. For the first time, three of our beloved games – Angry Birds 2, Angry Birds Friends, and Angry Birds Dream Blast – are joining forces to support this incredible cause.
####  Leveling the Playing Field, One Game at a Time
SpecialEffect is a UK-based charity that uses the magic of technology to help people with physical disabilities play video games. They create bespoke control setups for individuals with a wide range of physical challenges, from those with cerebral palsy to people recovering from a stroke or a brain injury. Their team of occupational therapists and technical experts meet with a person, understand their specific needs, and then design and build a custom gaming solution. This could be anything from a modified joystick controlled by the chin, to a custom setup that uses the eyes to aim and shoot. See it all in action in the video below.
SpecialEffect is also a force for good within the games industry itself, sharing its expertise with developers to make games more accessible for everyone. Their consultations and workshops have influenced major players in the industry, contributing to the creation of accessible hardware like the Xbox Adaptive Controller and the Sony Access Controller. It’s a holistic approach that seeks to make the world of gaming a more inclusive place for all.
####  One Special Day: Three Games, One Purpose
On One Special Day, the gaming community unites to support SpecialEffect. It’s an annual event where studios, publishers, and players come together to make a difference. We’ve been a proud participant for many years, with  leading the charge. This year, we are thrilled to expand our support with  and  joining the flock.
Here’s how it works: **On Friday, October 3** , all proceeds from in-app purchases made in Angry Birds 2, Angry Birds Friends, and Angry Birds Dream Blast within the UK will be donated directly to SpecialEffect – meaning every UK purchase, every gem pack, every golden ticket, every bit of in-game fun you have on that day will go toward helping someone experience the joy of play.
The more games we have participating, the more players we can reach, and the greater impact we can make. We’ve seen firsthand the passion and generosity of our players, and we know that together, we can make this the most impactful One Special Day yet.
####  How You Can Help
It’s simple. On October 3, if you’re in the UK, simply play Angry Birds 2, Angry Birds Friends, or Angry Birds Dream Blast and make an in-app purchase. Whether you’re a long-time player or someone who just picked up the game, your contribution will make a real difference. Each purchase helps SpecialEffect continue its invaluable work, providing bespoke solutions that change lives.
If you’re not in the UK, you can still support SpecialEffect by  or by . We encourage you to spread the word about One Special Day. Let’s show the world the power of the gaming community when we come together for a common cause. Together, we can make this a truly special day!
