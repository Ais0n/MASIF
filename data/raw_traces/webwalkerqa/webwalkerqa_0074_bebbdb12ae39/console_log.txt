### Plains
The open flatlands are rich in plant resources, low level animals and NPCs. The terrain here is easy to navigate, it is the perfect training place for beginners, but it can be difficult to hide your base here.
### Swamp
Plagued with Miasma and venomous bugs, this kind of wetland is one of the most dangerous habitats in the world. However, the availability of bugs and high level plant resources here makes this place a popular destination for explorers. Bring a censer, at least then you can breathe.
### Rainforest
Extremely rich in fauna and flora. The thick vegetation makes riding and vehicle travel difficult, but it does make hiding easy, which is why this can be an ideal location for beginners.
### Alpine
In the snow covered land of the extreme cold, where only Fir Trees can survive the blizzards. The mines here contain the most precious mineral resources in the world. Pay attention to keeping warm when you exploring here.
### Gobi
An extremely hot arid region, only Poplar Trees and a few shrubs grow here. The False Daisy is a precious plant that is unique to this habitat. Remember to bring an Ice Container to cool down and beware of dust storms.
### Mountains
Although the mountains make transportation difficult, they can become a natural barrier of defense. With relatively rich resources, they can meet the daily needs of novice players and can become a very popular area.

■ Guild: At the end of the season, the top 10 Guilds across the server will share a total reward pool of 8.88 million Gold Tokens.

■ Merit Rewards: Guild Leaders of other top-ranking Guilds will receive rare orb rewards.

## Myth of Empires × Myth of Empires: Throne Contribution Linkage System Preview
Generals,
Myth of Empires: Throne is scheduled to officially launch on April 16 at 13:00 UTC, with the Steam version opening for download and character creation at 8:00 UTC on the same day.
In response to player feedback and suggestions collected by Celestial Star Publishing, we have developed a Contribution Linkage system. After careful consideration, we are pleased to confirm that Myth of Empires will be functionally linked with Contribution Points generated during the seasonal gameplay of Myth of Empires: Throne.
This announcement serves as an initial preview. Detailed rules and the exact release schedule will be shared in future official updates.
**I. Linkage Rules Overview**
During Season 1 of Myth of Empires: Throne, players will earn Season Contribution based on their Season Points, calculated at a set ratio. These Season Contribution will be granted to the player’s linked Myth of Empires official server account.
Important:
This linkage system provides additional rewards only and will not consume any Season Points earned in Myth of Empires: Throne.
**II. Season Contribution Usage**
Season Contribution can be used in the Seasonal Contribution Shop in Myth of Empires to redeem various in-game items.
The Shop will be continuously expanded with additional rewards to enhance the cross-game experience.
Season Contribution will be calculated and distributed in real time throughout the season.
**III. Claiming & Account Binding**
A dedicated Contribution Claim & Binding page will be launched soon, allowing players to link their Myth of Empires: Throne account with their Myth of Empires account.
All players, whether using the official client or Steam, must complete this binding process to receive Contribution Points.
Each Myth of Empires account can be linked to only one Myth of Empires: Throne account. Once confirmed, the binding cannot be changed, so please proceed carefully.
**IV. Availability**
The Contribution Claim & Binding feature page is currently under development and will be released in the near future. Further details, including access and full rules, will be announced soon.
Thank you for your continued support of both Myth of Empires and Myth of Empires: Throne. We look forward to delivering a more connected and rewarding dual-version gameplay experience.
Myth of Empires Operations Team

## Myth of Empires: Xizhou Civilization DLC is Now Live – S4 Season Begins!
Developed by Angela Game and published by Imperium Interactive Entertainment, the cold weapon warfare sandbox MMO Myth of Empires has officially launched the Xizhou Civilization DLC on March 21 at 02:00 UTC.

Within two weeks of the Xizhou Civilization DLC launch, players can enjoy a 10% discount on the Xizhou Civilization Pack. Additionally, from March 20 at 17:00 UTC to April 3 at 17:00 UTC, there will be discounts on several items: the Myth of Empires base game and Dongzhou Map will be 65% off, the Hellenic Civilization Pack will be 40% off, and both the Musical Instruments Pack and the Royal Carriages Pack will be 50% off.

Hidden within the map are secrets to immortality and rare resources, which will help players craft unique armor and weapons. These treasures have attracted the attention of various factions, leading to a land filled with undercurrents, danger, and intrigue.
!

Furthermore, five powerful vehicles and devices—Aerial Fortress, Desert Ship, Navigator, Mechanized Woodcutter, and Mechanized Horse—will each serve their purpose, offering new options for combat, resource gathering, and more.
!

## Myth of Empires Xizhou Civilization DLC introduces the Aerial Fortress, the airborne dominator that reshapes the battlefield.
Myth of Empires Xizhou Civilization DLC is now in the final countdown and will officially launch on March 21 at 02:00 UTC, with servers going live at 14:00 UTC the same day. This DLC is packed with content, including previously revealed exciting features and a game-changing surprise—the Aerial Fortress. This colossal airborne behemoth will reshape the battlefield, becoming a decisive force in the hands of players to turn the tide of war.
!
This is a costly airborne mobile platform and a meticulously crafted strategic weapon. Flying higher than a giant Kongming lantern, it provides a commanding aerial view of the battlefield. With a fortress defense level of 75 and enhanced durability, it truly lives up to the name Aerial Fortress. However, its massive size comes with drawbacks—relatively slow movement speed and limited maneuverability, making quick turns difficult. In the ever-changing tides of battle, this could be a significant weakness.
In terms of functionality, the Aerial Fortress is not only capable of supporting structures but also allows players to equip it with various siege weapons, including Heavy Ballista, Mechanical Crossbow, Repeating Crossbow, and Counterweight Trebuchet. While airborne, it can launch attacks on targets below. However, for balance reasons, the number of weapons that can be mounted is strictly limited.
In the upcoming Xizhou map, the striking Yadan landforms will draw countless adventurers to explore. These towering wind-eroded pillars, with broad tops and narrow bases, are prime locations for many guilds to establish their strongholds. Their natural terrain advantages make them nearly inaccessible by land, leaving enemy forces with no choice but to watch from afar. In this scenario, the Aerial Fortress becomes the ultimate siege weapon. In PVP mode, its arrival will disrupt the traditional ground-based warfare, enabling high-altitude assaults that strike deep into enemy territory, delivering devastating blows and completely reshaping the battlefield dynamics.
Of course, the Aerial Fortress is not invincible. In the game's battlefield ecosystem, watchtowers and ballista serve as effective counters, especially when attacks precisely hit the balloon on top of the Aerial Fortress, dealing triple damage and inflicting significant damage.
With the upcoming update to the Xizhou Civilization DLC, the powerful addition of the Aerial Fortress will undoubtedly inject new vitality into the game, offering players an unprecedented fresh experience. On March 21, we look forward to stepping into the new world of Myth of Empires together, embarking on an unforgettable journey of exploration and conquest!
