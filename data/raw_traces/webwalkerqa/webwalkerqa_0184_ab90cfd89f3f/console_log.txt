# From Honolulu and Beyond—Thank You for Shaping What’s Next
The **2026 MRS Spring Meeting & Exhibit**, held April 26–May 1 in Honolulu, Hawai‘i, brought together materials researchers from academia, industry, government and national laboratories for a week of cross-disciplinary collaboration and scientific exchange. Set against one of MRS’s most inspiring locations, the Meeting highlighted advances in sustainable manufacturing, energy materials and emerging technologies shaping the future of the field.
  
Spanning four official venues, the Meeting distributed programming, events and accommodations throughout Honolulu’s walkable, picturesque layout. This interconnected format supported engagement across symposia, sessions, networking events and exhibit hall activities, creating a focused and collaborative environment for research and dialogue.
  
Technical sessions reflected the field’s momentum, with topics including AI-driven materials discovery, semiconductor technologies, photonics and quantum materials, battery innovation and energy conversion, additive manufacturing, and bioelectronics and sustainable material systems.
  
Beyond the technical program, attendees engaged in networking, career development and community-driven events, while the exhibit hall showcased tools, technologies and publishing resources. Together, these experiences underscored the Meeting’s role as a platform for discovery, collaboration and continued innovation in materials research.
