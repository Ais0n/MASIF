###  Our vision for sustainability.
We want to champion sustainable gaming through our actions—crafting safe and joyful play experiences, fueling creativity with an inclusive and empowering culture, and protecting our planet.
      
###  Our Strategy.
Our sustainability strategy focuses on three areas: Positive Play, Empowered People, and Protecting our Planet. The selection of focus areas is based on our company strategy, mission, and values and a 2024 sustainability double materiality analysis.
!
#### Positive Play
We champion responsible, safe, and enjoyable gaming experiences, ensuring positivity in every moment of play.
!
#### Empowered People
We empower Rovians to unlock their full potential and unique talents, crafting games that connect with our diverse players worldwide.
!
#### Protecting our Planet
We protect our planet by making sustainable choices throughout our value chain and amplifying our positive impact by raising awareness.
###  Key Partnerships.
  * #### Playing for the Planet
Since 2019, the Playing for the Planet Alliance has played a key role in driving better climate and environmental management within the gaming industry. Rovio has been an active Alliance member since its launch. The Alliance is led by the United Nations Environment. Programme (UNEP). The Alliance provides valuable member support, has different working groups, and raises awareness while supporting members in media coverage. Playing for the Planet has over 50 members today.
  * #### FIBS
FIBS (Finnish Business & Society) have been helping Finnish companies integrate responsibility and business on a practical level since 2000. It is the largest corporate responsibility network in the Nordic countries. The purpose of FIBS is to accelerate changemakers towards a more sustainable world. FIBS aims to help companies achieve more sustainable business practices that consider the planet’s needs and boundaries. Rovio has been a FIBS member since 2014.
  * #### Better Games Together
Better Games Together (BGT) is a collective of Finnish game studios with the shared goal of making the game industry safer and more accepting of LGBTQIA+ people and their identities. We’ve been a member of BGT since 2023. By working together, we can learn together, share learnings, create a bigger impact, and help LGBTQIA+ employees connect throughout the Finnish games industry.
